KickLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-13 · Last updated: 2026-10-13

KickLog records soccer matches with Apple Watch. We run no servers of our own, show no ads, do not track you, and never sell data. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

1. Data we use

Match and health data

  • Location (distance, route, heat map), heart rate, steps, and speed recorded by Apple Watch during a match.
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, date of birth, sex, body mass, running speed, steps, and past Soccer workouts read from Health, used to calculate match stats, heart-rate zones, calories, and Match Form.
  • Each match is saved to Health as a Soccer workout.
  • Data read from Health is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared with third parties.

Profile and settings

  • Nickname, avatar, age, body mass, preferred position, saved pitches, and app settings.

iPhone location (only when Pitch Arrival Reminders are on)

  • The system checks on your iPhone whether you have arrived at a saved or regular pitch. Pitch recognition runs on your iPhone only; KickLog does not look up pitches online.

Photos and local network

  • Photos access is used only to save share cards you create.
  • Local network, Bluetooth, and Wi‑Fi are used only while the Face to Face screen is open, to find friends next to you. Only your nickname, a small avatar thumbnail, an irreversible account hash, and a one-time token are exchanged, encrypted end to end.

2. Where data is stored

  • On your devices.
  • In your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), to sync between your devices and restore after reinstalling. We cannot access your iCloud content.
  • In backup files you export yourself.
  • About "expiry" of records in Apple's CloudKit public database: KickLog has no servers and no scheduled cleanup. Once a record expires, the recipient stops reading it. It is actually deleted only when the sender's device opens the app after expiry, so deletion may happen later than the expiry time. If the sender never opens the app again, the encrypted or hashed record may remain in Apple's public database. All times below work this way.

3. Sharing with friends

When you add a friend, your friend card is shared through iCloud with the friends you have shared it with. Only they can see:

  • Your nickname, avatar, and preferred position.
  • Current and past season totals: matches, distance, time played, sprints, top speed, best score, and badge count.
  • Only after you turn on Detect Matches Together (off by default): the start time of up to 5 matches from the last 14 days and the approximate pitch location (about 100 m). Routes and precise coordinates are never shared.
  • Friends you tag as playing together: the match time, their account identifier, and your distance, sprints, top speed, and score for that match.

You can stop sharing at any time:

  • Turn off Detect Matches Together on the Friends screen to stop sharing match times and pitch locations; any already shared are removed.
  • Remove a played-together tag to delete that record.
  • Remove a friend to stop sending them updates. Their app will tell them you removed them and keep the data you last synced; they decide whether to delete it. To notify them, a temporary record is left in Apple's CloudKit public database. It holds only a salted hash of their account and expires after 24 hours. Your device deletes it about 24 hours after it expires, so it is usually deleted after about 2 days. Data already cached on their device cannot be deleted by us.
  • Friend data is kept separate for each Apple ID.

Only send your invite link to the person you want to add.

4. Sharing with your team

Team data is stored in the captain's iCloud shared zone (Apple CloudKit). KickLog has no servers. All team members can see:

  • Team name, color, badge, the captain's user ID, whether the captain's membership is active, and creation time.
  • For each member: user ID, nickname, avatar, preferred position, and current-season matches, distance, time played, sprints, top speed, best score, and update time.

Not shared with your team: badges, location, heat map, individual matches, and past seasons.

Invites

  • Only the captain can invite. Before you join, the app tells you your data will be visible to the whole team.
  • An in-person QR code is valid for 10 minutes and works once. A sent link is valid for 7 days and works once. Regenerating the link cancels all unused links.
  • Invite tickets are stored in the captain's private iCloud database.
  • The captain can also invite a friend directly. The invite leaves one temporary record in Apple's CloudKit public database. It holds only a salted hash of the invitee's account and encrypted invite info (the team, team name, captain's nickname, and share link), with no readable account or name. The invite link works only for the invitee's account and expires after 7 days. After expiry, the captain's device clears invites that get no response. Canceling an invite deletes this record.
  • If the invitee declines, a temporary record that expires after about 24 hours is left in the public database. It holds only a salted hash of the captain's account and encrypted decline info (the team and the decliner's account and nickname), with no readable account or name. Invite and decline alerts are local notifications on your device, not server push notifications.
  • Temporary records in Apple's CloudKit public database hold only hashes or salted hashes, encrypted invite info, and an expiry time. They contain no readable account, nickname, or team name. The one exception is the member-removal notice: it holds a salted hash of the removed member's account and the team ID in plain text (a random UUID, not encrypted). It expires after 24 hours and is deleted by the captain's device on its next refresh after expiry. Invite and request records also carry short expiry times.

Team match tags and team match reports stay on your device only.

Leaving and dissolving

  • When you leave a team, your published data is deleted first. If that fails, it is retried.
  • When the captain removes a member, the captain deletes that member's data.
  • If the captain leaves, the team is dissolved and the whole team zone is deleted.

5. Data sent to Apple

  • Weather: to show weather in match reports, we send the approximate area of the pitch (about 1 km) and the kickoff time window to Apple WeatherKit. Routes and precise locations are never sent.
  • Maps: the heat map satellite view and the share long image load map tiles from Apple Maps for the area around the pitch. This request reveals the approximate area shown on the map to Apple and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. KickLog does not store this request or send it anywhere else.
  • iCloud sync, friend sharing, and team sharing run on Apple CloudKit and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • We use no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect children's contact information.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete matches or friends in the app.
  • Leaving a team deletes the data you published to it; if the captain leaves, the team is dissolved.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the device.
  • Delete KickLog's iCloud data in iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
  • Workouts saved to Health can be deleted in the Health app.

8. Changes and contact

We will update the effective date and notify you in the app when this policy changes materially. Questions? Email kicklogsupport@gmail.com.
