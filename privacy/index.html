KickLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-13 · Last updated: 2026-10-15

KickLog records soccer matches with Apple Watch. We run no servers of our own, show no ads, do not track you, and never sell data. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

1. Data we use

Match and health data

  • Location (distance, route, heat map), heart rate, steps, and speed recorded by Apple Watch during a match.
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate, date of birth, sex, body mass, running speed, steps, and past Soccer workouts read from Health, used to calculate match stats, heart-rate zones, calories, and Match Form.
  • You can import past Soccer workouts from Health into KickLog sessions on your device. If an import overlaps a Watch recording, the Watch session is kept. Imported sessions may lack stints or attack direction and are marked incomplete.
  • Each match is saved to Health as a Soccer workout.
  • Data read from Health is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared with third parties.

Profile and settings

  • Nickname, avatar, age, body mass, preferred position, saved pitches, and app settings.

iPhone location

  • While Using: to recognize your pitch, support local weather lookups (WeatherKit receives only an about 1 km pitch cell derived from the match, not continuous tracking), and show satellite maps around the pitch. Match track and distance are still recorded on Apple Watch.
  • Always (only if you turn on Pitch Arrival Reminders): the system checks on your iPhone whether you have arrived at a saved pitch and can remind you to start recording. That check happens on your iPhone; KickLog has no server of its own that receives this location. KickLog does not look up pitches online.

Photos and local network

  • Photos access is used only to save share cards you create.
  • Local network, Bluetooth, and Wi‑Fi are used only while the Face to Face screen is open, to find friends next to you. Only your nickname, a small avatar thumbnail, an irreversible code derived from your account, and a one-time token are exchanged, encrypted end to end.

2. Where data is stored

  • On your devices.
  • In your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), to sync between your devices and restore after reinstalling. We cannot access your iCloud content.
  • In backup files you export yourself. Manual backups mainly include your match sessions. Your message list and some on-device helper state may not be included; check what the export screen lists when you create a backup.
  • Message center notices: to let someone know about a new friend, a teammate joining or leaving, a team being dissolved, a captain cancelling an invite, a declined invite, being removed from a team, being removed as a friend, or a withdrawn played-together tag, KickLog leaves a temporary record in Apple's CloudKit public database. The only publicly readable parts are an irreversible code representing the recipient (derived from their account identifier or team ID; it can't be turned back into either) and an expiry time. Everything else is encrypted and readable only by the recipient: the notice type and the sender's account identifier and nickname; for team notices, the team ID and team name; for withdrawal notices, the match start time, the original tag time (if any), and the withdrawal time. A withdrawal notice goes only to the friend it concerns. Records expire after up to 14 days (14 days for withdrawal notices, 7 days for others) and are then deleted by the sender's device. Apple uses the record's built-in creator information to verify the sender. Withdrawal notices not yet sent wait in a queue on your device (friend ID, match start, tag time, and withdrawal time; up to 60 entries, kept 14 days, deleted once sent).
  • Your message center list is stored only on your device, not in iCloud.
  • About "expiry" of records in Apple's CloudKit public database: KickLog has no servers and no scheduled cleanup. Once a record expires, the recipient stops reading it. It is actually deleted only when the sender's device opens the app after expiry, so deletion may happen later than the expiry time. If the sender never opens the app again, the encrypted or encoded record may remain in Apple's public database. All times below work this way.

3. Sharing with friends

When you add a friend, your friend card is shared through iCloud with the friends you have shared it with. Only they can see:

  • Your nickname, avatar, and preferred position.
  • Current and past season totals: matches, distance, time played, sprints, top speed, best score, and badge count.
  • Only after you turn on Detect Matches Together (off by default): the start time of up to 5 matches from the last 14 days and the approximate pitch location (about 100 m). Routes and precise coordinates are never shared.
  • Friends you tag as playing together: the match time, their account identifier, and your distance, sprints, top speed, and score for that match.

You can stop sharing at any time:

  • Turn off Detect Matches Together on the Friends screen to stop sharing match times and pitch locations; any already shared are removed.
  • Remove a played-together tag to delete that record. The tag on your friend's side is withdrawn automatically, and they get a message center notice.
  • Remove a friend to stop sending them updates. Their app will tell them you removed them and keep the data you last synced; they decide whether to delete it. To notify them, a temporary record is left in Apple's CloudKit public database. It holds only an irreversible code derived from their account and expires after 24 hours. Your device deletes it about 24 hours after it expires, so it is usually deleted after about 2 days. Data already cached on their device cannot be deleted by us.
  • Friend and team data are tied to the iCloud account you are signed into. If you switch or sign out of iCloud, you may temporarily not see friends from the previous account. Avoid switching accounts without a backup. We are improving per-account isolation and restore.

Only send your invite link to the person you want to add.

Adding friends

  • Face to Face, QR code, or link invites are one-time (a link is short-lived and works only once). Send them only to the person you want to add.
  • While adding a friend, KickLog may leave temporary records in Apple's CloudKit public database. What is publicly readable is mainly irreversible codes and an expiry time; the rest is encrypted. After expiry, the sender's device deletes them (timing follows the expiry note above).
  • If a mutual add is interrupted, KickLog may put someone who already entered the handshake back on your friend share without you tapping Add Friend again, so the handshake can finish. Someone you deliberately removed is not added back automatically.
  • Friend requests and confirmations may show a lock-screen notification with fixed text that contains no nickname or account.

4. Sharing with your team

Team details and the roster live in the captain's iCloud (an Apple CloudKit shared zone); members can read but not change them. Each member's season stats and photo are stored by that member in Apple's CloudKit public database, encrypted so only teammates can open them; anyone else sees only scrambled data. Record names use irreversible codes only, with no account or name. Apple automatically notes which account created each record (an anonymous, app-specific ID); we use it to confirm the data came from a team member. KickLog has no servers. All team members can see:

  • Team name, color, badge, the captain's user ID, whether the captain's membership is active, and creation time, plus content fingerprints of the badge and member avatar images (used only to tell whether an image has changed).
  • For each member: user ID, nickname, avatar, preferred position, and current-season matches, distance, time played, sprints, top speed, best score, and update time.

The roster lists the account IDs of members and of people invited but not yet joined; only the captain can edit it, and team members can see it.

Your photo is cropped on your phone to a small square (max 64 KB), encrypted for teammates only, and uploaded only when it changes.

Not shared with your team: badges, location, heat map, individual matches, and past seasons.

Invites

  • Only the captain can invite. Before you join, the app tells you your data will be visible to the whole team.
  • An in-person QR code is valid for 10 minutes and works once. A sent link is valid for 7 days and works once. Regenerating the link cancels all unused links.
  • Invite tickets are stored in the captain's private iCloud database.
  • Invite links hold only the captain's account ID, a one-time random code and an expiry time, with no team info; the team info is sent to you in an encrypted reply after you join.
  • The captain can also invite a friend directly. The invite leaves one temporary record in Apple's CloudKit public database. It holds only an irreversible code derived from the invitee's account and encrypted invite info (the team, team name, captain's nickname, and share link), with no readable account or name. The invite link works only for the invitee's account and expires after 7 days. After expiry, the captain's device clears invites that get no response. Canceling an invite deletes this record.
  • If the invitee declines, a temporary record that expires after about 24 hours is left in the public database. It holds only an irreversible code derived from the captain's account and encrypted decline info (the team, the decliner's account and nickname, and which kind of reply it is), with no readable account or name. Invite and decline alerts are local notifications on your device, not server push notifications.
  • Temporary records in Apple's CloudKit public database hold only irreversible codes, encrypted info, and an expiry time. They contain no readable account, nickname, or team name. The member-removal notice holds only an irreversible code derived from the removed member's account and the team ID in encrypted form (only team members can decrypt it); the team ID is no longer stored in plain text. It expires after 24 hours and is deleted by the captain's device on its next refresh after expiry. Invite and request records also carry short expiry times.
  • When the captain replies to a join request, the reply is matched to your request only by an irreversible code derived from a random record name, and the team name is kept in the encrypted part.
  • When the captain dissolves a team, a dissolve notice is left in the public database. It holds only an irreversible code that can't be turned back into the team ID and an expiry time (7 days), with no names and no plain-text IDs. The creator and creation time that Apple attaches to every record are used to confirm the notice really came from the captain.
  • Every team-related record in the public database carries an ownership tag so your device can later find and clean up the records it sent. The tag is generated daily from a key in your own iCloud and changes every day; it cannot be used to identify you.
  • There are 5 kinds of team lock-screen push notifications. All use fixed text and contain no nicknames or team names.

Team match tags and team match reports stay on your device only. So does the helper data used to send, receive, and clean up notices: team notice subscriptions, whether a dissolve notice was sent or handled, pending dissolve notices (with record name and team ID), team zone sync state, a captain quota flag, ownership-tag recovery and cleanup times, and a list of public records waiting to be cleaned up.

Leaving and dissolving

  • When you leave a team, your published data is deleted first. If that fails, it is retried.
  • Removing a member deletes no one's data automatically. The removed member's earlier data is no longer shown to the team, and the captain changes the encryption so they can't see later team data. After removal, you may still see anonymous record IDs, counts and update times for current members in the public database, but not their stats or photos (still encrypted; you no longer have the team key).
  • After the captain removes you, or a team is dissolved, your iPhone keeps a "Removed from Team" / "Team Dissolved" card. You can tap "Delete My Data in This Team" to delete only your own encrypted season stats and photo from the public database. The name card can stay or be removed by you. Older frozen cards that never stored your record names may not offer this delete control; ciphertext nobody can decrypt may remain until Apple expires or removes it.
  • If the captain leaves, the team is dissolved. Dissolving deletes the team details, the roster and the captain's own data in the public database. Other members' encrypted data stays in the public database, but what's needed to open it is deleted with the team, so no one can read it and it is no longer shown.

5. Data sent to Apple

  • Weather: to show weather in match reports, we send the approximate area of the pitch (about 1 km) and the kickoff time window to Apple WeatherKit. Routes and precise locations are never sent.
  • Maps: the heat map satellite view and the share long image load map tiles from Apple Maps for the area around the pitch. This request reveals the approximate area shown on the map to Apple and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. KickLog does not store this request or send it anywhere else.
  • iCloud sync, friend sharing, and team sharing run on Apple CloudKit and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Push notifications: to receive message center notices promptly, the app creates a CloudKit subscription for your recipient code, and Apple delivers the push notifications.
  • We use no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect children's contact information.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete matches or friends in the app.
  • Leaving a team deletes the data you published to it; if the captain leaves, the team is dissolved. Removing a member does not automatically delete that member's data; the removed member can delete their own team stats and photo from the "Removed from Team" or "Team Dissolved" card.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the device.
  • Delete KickLog's iCloud data in iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.
  • Workouts saved to Health can be deleted in the Health app.

8. Changes and contact

We will update the effective date and notify you in the app when this policy changes materially. Questions? Email kicklogsupport@gmail.com.
